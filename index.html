Designer story

A studio language built on love.

Marrolo started as a school project and quickly became my creative world.

What began as a place to share my designs and build a portfolio turned into something much bigger when people started connecting with my work. Through fashion, I found a way to express emotions, tell stories, and create pieces that make people feel confident and seen.

As Marrolo grew, I expanded from one-of-one designs into collections and merchandise, collaborating with magazines, artists, musicians, DJs, singers, and creatives who wanted something unique for their performances, projects, and everyday style.

Today, Marrolo moves between Mexico and Canada, blending designer fashion with street culture through seasonal collections, custom pieces, and limited-edition merch. Every collection reflects a different feeling, moment, or idea, while staying true to the brand's core: self-expression, creativity, and individuality.

For me, Marrolo has never been just about clothing. It's about creating pieces that people can make their own and building a community that isn't afraid to stand out.

And we're just getting started.
